Our story

Growing a fresher, fairer food connection

Kampala Fresh Produce Direct is a farmer cooperative connecting peri-urban growers with households, restaurants, hotels and small businesses in Kampala.

Grown close to home From our member farmers to your table.
Who we are

Farmers working together

We bring together farmers from Kasangati, Gayaza and Mukono to make fresh produce easier to find and sell. Our members grow tomatoes, onions, cabbages, matooke, potatoes, carrots, green pepper, fruits and leafy vegetables.

Customers can discover available produce, see prices and quantities, then place an order through one simple online experience.

  • ✓Closer connections between growers and customers
  • ✓Clearer information about products, prices and quantities
  • ✓More organized ordering and delivery details
